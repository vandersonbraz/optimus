Optimus Player

A clean, fast media player for your Samsung Smart TV.

Optimus Player lets you load your own media playlists and enjoy your personal video collection on the big screen, with a simple interface built for the TV remote.

Features

  • Add and play your own media playlists
  • Content organized into Channels, Movies and Series
  • Fast search and favorites
  • Resume playback from where you stopped
  • Parental control and multi-language interface

Optimus Player does not include any content. You use your own playlist to watch the media you already own.
